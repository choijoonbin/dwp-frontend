# DWP Brand Mark Exploration

Status: concept review only. None of these assets is wired into the product.

## Brand premise

DWP is a governed agentic work platform. The mark should communicate controlled
action and connected work without depending on an AI sparkle, a generic chatbot,
or a decorative glass effect.

The identity is split into two layers:

1. **Master mark**: flat vector artwork that remains recognizable at 16 px, in one
   color, and on light or dark surfaces.
2. **App icon treatment**: the master mark placed on a platform-appropriate
   container. Material, depth, and Liquid Glass effects belong here.

## Directions

### A. Governed Flow (recommended)

Three work streams converge through a mint control gate and leave as one governed
action. The streams represent people, enterprise knowledge, and systems or agents;
the gate represents policy, approval, audit, and safe execution. It is the strongest
product-story fit and avoids the crowded field of generic D monograms.

### B. Work Weave

One continuous W ribbon joins two work paths at a mint collaboration point. It is
friendly and memorable, but expresses workplace collaboration more strongly than
governance.

### C. DWP Monogram

A compact custom acronym preserves the recognition of the current tile while
removing the dependency on a UI font. It is the lowest-risk evolution, but it is
less ownable and loses clarity fastest at favicon size.

## Review gates before production

- Select one direction and refine geometry on an optical grid.
- Test single-color, reversed, 16/24/32/64 px, and high-contrast variants.
- Build horizontal and compact lockups with the Digital Workplace wordmark.
- Run a professional trademark and visual-similarity clearance search.
- Export outlined SVG masters and platform-specific app icon layers.
